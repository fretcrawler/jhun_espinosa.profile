Trading Bot Multi-Exchange Platform

Automated trading integration across Binance, OKX, and BingX.

Overview

Developed a personal multi-exchange trading bot platform that ingests strategy signals, executes orders across connected exchanges, and centralizes monitoring for bot health and performance.

Scope

  • Exchange connector integrations for Binance, OKX, and BingX
  • Unified signal processing and order execution pipeline
  • Risk controls for position sizing, stops, and exposure limits
  • Operational dashboard for status, logs, and exception monitoring

Business Value

  • Single control layer for multi-exchange strategy execution
  • Reduced manual trade operations and decision latency
  • More consistent application of trading and risk rules
