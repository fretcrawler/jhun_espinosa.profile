Centralized execution, monitoring, and governance for multi-team software delivery.

Overview

Developed a project management system that unifies planning, sprint execution, issue tracking, and status reporting to improve decision-making and delivery predictability.

Scope

  • Requirements and workflow mapping with stakeholders
  • Feature modules for planning, assignments, and milestones
  • Progress dashboards and risk visibility
  • Standardized governance and reporting routines

Business Value

  • Better cross-functional alignment and transparency
  • Faster issue escalation and resolution
  • More consistent project execution cadence
